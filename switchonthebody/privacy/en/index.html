Privacy Policy

SwitchOn Body ("the app") explains below how it handles your personal information.

  • Effective date: October 18, 2026
  • Contact: ideality.switchonthebody@gmail.com

1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not run any server and does not collect or receive your personal information. There is no sign-up or login.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following information is stored only on your device for app features and is never sent to anyone, including the developer.

InformationPurpose
Age, sex, height, weight, usual activityCalculating daily calorie, protein and water targets
Wake, meal, workout and bedtime times; program start dateBuilding your daily schedule and reminders
Completed tasks, weight recordsShowing your progress and weight trend
Body photos (RealBody)Comparing photos taken in the same frame
  • Body photos are kept only in the app's private storage. They are never saved to your gallery or sent anywhere.
  • Retention and deletion: Everything is deleted immediately when you tap "Settings > Reset all data" or uninstall the app. You can also delete photos one by one.

3. Information collected by the ad SDK (third party)

The app uses Google AdMob to stay free. AdMob collects the following information directly from your device to serve ads. The developer cannot receive or see this information.

Collected byInformationPurpose
Google LLC (AdMob)Advertising ID (device identifier), IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device model and OS, app interactions such as ad views and clicks, diagnosticsServing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud
  • International transfer: This information is sent over the network to Google LLC servers (USA) while you use the app and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Opting out of personalized ads: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before ads load, and can change their choice at any time in "Settings > Ad privacy settings".

4. Processors and third-party sharing

Because the developer does not collect personal information, it does not use processors or share information with third parties. Collection by the ad SDK is described in section 3.

6. Feedback email

If you send an email from "Settings > Send feedback", we use your message and the app version, OS and device model added to it only to handle your inquiry. We delete it without delay once the inquiry is resolved, and keep it for up to 1 year only when needed to handle a dispute.

7. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete the information stored on your device in the app at any time. Send any privacy questions to the contact below.

8. Audience

This app is for adults aged 19 and over. We do not collect information from children under 14.
